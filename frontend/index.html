YOUR FIRST NIGHT IN THE VILLAGE

狼人殺，怎樣玩？How to play Werewolf

這是同場遊戲的主持工具：一部裝置交接操作，大家在現實中閉眼、發言及投票。This app hosts an in-person game. Pass one device between players while everyone closes their eyes, discusses and votes in real life.

01 · 遊戲流程The game flow

  1. 設定 5–16 位玩家及房規，開始後按大號碼依次私下查看身份，再按確認交給下一位。Choose 5–16 players and agree on house rules. Follow the large seat number, privately reveal your role, then confirm and pass the device.
  2. 夜晚跟隨主持語音閉眼。被叫到的角色才可單獨看私人畫面；同角色有多人時，按座位順序輪流操作。At night, follow the narration and close your eyes. Only the called role views the private screen. Multiple holders of a role act one at a time in seat order.
  3. 天亮公布死亡，處理獵人的最後一槍，再輪流發言、找出壞人。At dawn, announce deaths and resolve any hunter shots, then discuss and look for the bad side.
  4. 每位存活玩家一票，可投自己或棄權。平票只加賽一次，再平票或全體棄權則無人出局。現場已點票可使用「直接輸入結果」，平票時選全部最高票玩家。Each living player has one vote and may vote for themselves or abstain. Hold one runoff for a tie; another tie or all abstentions means no elimination. If votes were counted in person, use the counted-result option and select every tied leader.
  5. 所有狼人出局則好人勝；預設狼人數目不少於好人則狼人勝。獵人連鎖行動全部完成後才結算，雙方全滅為平局。The good side wins when all wolves are eliminated. By default, wolves win at parity with the good side. Resolve every hunter shot before checking victory. Both sides eliminated means a draw.

02 · 每個角色的能力What each role does

狼人 · Werewolf

壞人陣營。每晚共同選一位存活目標，可選狼人；是否能空刀由房規決定。白天隱藏身份並影響投票。Bad side. Together choose a living target each night, including a wolf if desired. Skipping the kill depends on the house rule. Hide your role and influence daytime votes.

預言家 · Seer

好人陣營。每晚查驗另一位存活玩家，只會知道「壞人陣營」或「好人陣營」，不會知道確切角色。結果只在私人畫面顯示。Good side. Investigate another living player each night. Learn only “Bad side” or “Good side,” never their exact role. Results stay private.

女巫 · Witch

好人陣營。每位女巫全局各有一瓶解藥及毒藥；每晚最多用一瓶或可同晚用雙藥，由房規決定。解藥救狼刀目標；毒藥可選任意存活玩家。能否自救、被刀當晚能否行動，由房規決定。Good side. Each witch has one antidote and one poison for the game. House rules decide whether she may use one or both on a night. Save the wolves’ target or poison any living player. Self-saving and acting when attacked depend on the house rules.

獵人 · Hunter

好人陣營。被狼人、投票或另一位獵人帶走時，可帶走一位存活玩家，也可放棄；被毒殺不能開槍。最後一槍不會自動略過。Good side. After a wolf kill, vote or hunter shot, shoot one living player or decline. Poison prevents your shot. Your final decision is never skipped automatically.

平民 · Villager

好人陣營。沒有夜間能力；聆聽討論、分析線索，並用白天的一票找出狼人。Good side. You have no night power. Listen, evaluate clues and use your daytime vote to find the wolves.

03 · 房規及娛樂模式House rules and fun mode

標準模式的預言家、女巫及獵人各最多一位。娛樂模式可增加這些角色，例如一狼配多位獵人；每位女巫有獨立藥水。開局狼人仍必須少於好人。Standard mode allows at most one seer, witch and hunter. Fun mode allows more, such as one wolf with several hunters. Each witch has separate potions. Wolves must still be fewer than the good side at setup.

觀眾請使用「觀眾畫面」，不要分享主持人的瀏覽器。交接前先關閉 DEBUG，避免公開全部身份。Spectators should use the audience view, never the host browser. Turn DEBUG off before passing the device so all roles are not exposed.

04 · 誰有遺言？Who gets last words?

可在開局規則啟用遺言。首夜出局者（包括狼刀、毒藥及獵人連鎖）及每次被票出局者有遺言。白天被獵人帶走者沒有；第二晚起夜間出局者也沒有。所有獵人開槍處理完後，依序發表遺言，主持人確認後才結算勝負或進入下一階段。Enable last words in the house rules. First-night casualties (including wolf kills, poison and hunter chains) and every voted-out player get last words. Daytime hunter-shot victims and night casualties from night two onward do not. Resolve all hunter shots first, then take last words in order. The host confirms each speaker before victory or the next phase.

05 · 安裝到裝置Install on your device

iPhone／iPad：在 Safari 開啟網站，按分享，選「加入主畫面」，並開啟「作為網頁 App 打開」。Android、Windows 及 Mac：使用支援安裝的瀏覽器，在網址列或選單選擇安裝。遊戲需要網絡；請先安裝，再於 App 內開局。iPhone/iPad: open the site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and enable Open as Web App. Android, Windows and Mac: use your browser’s install option in its address bar or menu. Play requires an internet connection. Install first, then create your game inside the app.
